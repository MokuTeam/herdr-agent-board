❯ 1. Move them to an archive folder (Recommended)
     I make a folder beside the library (\PHOTOS_archive_spring) and move the 1300 pictures there instead of
     deleting them. They leave the library, stay exactly as they are, and can be moved back. We remove the
     folder later with a separate OK. Downside: one more folder in the library, ben should know about it.
  2. Delete them, keep a list
     anna says she can sync them again if needed, the album has them all. I delete them as planned, and the copy
     keeps only a list (name, size, date taken), without the pictures. Cannot be undone.
  3. A one-off export job for the zip
     I set up a one-off export job that pulls every picture, build the zip on this machine, then delete the
     pictures and the job. Exactly what cleo asked for, but one more job to clean up and about an hour of work.
